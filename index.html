Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Dairova respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Dairova mobile application.

Privacy first: Dairova is designed as an offline personal diary. Your diary entries are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to Dairova servers.

1. Information You Create

Dairova allows you to create and manage personal diary entries. Information stored inside the app may include:

  • Diary entry titles
  • Diary text and notes
  • Selected moods
  • Entry creation dates
  • Entry modification dates

This information is stored locally on your device.

2. Personal Information

Dairova does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or social media account in order to use the app.

3. Local Data Storage

Diary entries and related information are stored locally on your device using the application's local storage or database system.

Dairova does not currently provide cloud synchronization or remote backups.

Clearing the application's data or uninstalling the app may permanently remove your locally stored diary entries.

4. Data Collection

Dairova does not currently collect or transmit the contents of your diary entries to our servers.

The app is designed to perform its core diary functionality offline.

5. Data Sharing

Dairova does not sell, rent, or share your diary entries with advertisers or other third parties.

6. Advertising and Analytics

Dairova currently does not use third-party advertising services or analytics services to track your diary activity.

7. Permissions

Dairova aims to request only the permissions that are necessary for the app to function.

The core diary functionality does not require access to sensitive information such as your contacts, precise location, microphone, or camera.

8. Data Security

We take privacy seriously and design Dairova to minimize unnecessary data transmission.

Because diary data is stored locally on your device, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device, operating system, screen lock, and backups.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Your diary entries remain stored on your device until you choose to remove them.

You can delete your information by:

  • Deleting individual diary entries
  • Using the app's Delete All Entries option
  • Clearing the application's data
  • Uninstalling Dairova from your device

10. Children's Privacy

Dairova does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The application is designed as a general-purpose personal journaling tool.

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of mobile applications where appropriate.

11. Third-Party Services

If future versions of Dairova introduce optional third-party services, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, or other online functionality, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page together with an updated "Last updated" date.
